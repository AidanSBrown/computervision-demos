Stacking filters: from edges to shapes to two numbers

A convolutional neural network repeats the same steps: filter, keep the strong responses, and shrink. Each round the maps of pixels (neurons) get smaller, until the whole image is down to two numbers that feed the classifier in demo 4. Each map comes from a different filter looking for a different feature. Here the first layer finds edges and the second combines them into shapes: the lesion's outline, and the wiggles and patches in it. A deeper network keeps going, and its last layers find patterns that tend to go with melanoma. Hover over any map to see which patch of the image it depends on (its receptive field).
