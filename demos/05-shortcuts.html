Clever Hans: when a model learns the wrong clue

A real model, trained live in your browser on 600 synthetic lesion photos. In this "hospital", suspicious lesions usually get a mark before the photo is taken. The model is never told what the mark means. It only learns which pixels predict the label.

Real cases
  • Pen-ink marks on pathology slides biased a melanoma triage model (Sankarapandian et al., 2021).
  • COVID-19 chest X-ray models leaned on text markers, image edges and patient positioning (DeGrave et al., 2021).
  • A hip-fracture model could identify the scanner (AUC 1.00) and "priority" orders. On matched test sets its fracture AUC fell to about 0.52 (Badgeley et al., 2019).
