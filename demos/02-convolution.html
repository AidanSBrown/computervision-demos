A filter slides over the image to find edges

A 3 × 3 grid of weights (the filter, or kernel) slides across every position. At each stop it multiplies the 9 pixels under it by its 9 weights and adds them up. That number becomes a pixel of the output.

Why it finds edges: on areas of skin with little contrast the negative and positive weights cancel to about 0. Where dark and light colors meet they don't, so the output lights up along the lesion's border, enhancing the contrast.
